Music class FAQs

Quick answers about Crestwood Music School in Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad.

Where is Crestwood Music School located?

Crestwood Music School is at G1-003, Palm Resort, Raj Nagar Extension, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.

Which music classes are available in Raj Nagar?

We offer piano and keyboard, guitar, vocal, tabla, dholak and harmonium classes in Raj Nagar Extension, Ghaziabad.

When can I attend a trial class?

Trial classes are available Thursday from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM, Saturday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and Sunday from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Please confirm your slot in advance.
